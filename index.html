Our services

Complete care for dogs and cats

  • Wellness & Preventive CareExams, vaccinations, microchipping and parasite prevention for puppies, kittens, adults and seniors.
  • Surgery & DentistrySpay and neuter, soft-tissue surgery, anesthesia and complete dental care for dogs and cats.
  • Advanced DiagnosticsDigital radiology, ultrasound, endoscopy and in-house lab tests for fast, accurate answers.
  • Reproductive ServicesDr. Mary Sebzda, DVM, DACT, is board certified in theriogenology and offers evaluations for all types of breeding.
  • Boarding & Day BoardingA home away from home, with an attendant on duty 24 hours a day for every boarded and hospitalized pet.
  • Acupuncture & Laser TherapyGentle pain relief from our certified veterinary acupuncturists, Dr. Alexandria Clifton and Dr. Keely McLaughlin.
  • Emergency CareUrgent care for sudden illness and injury during our open hours, 7 days a week.
  • PharmacyGet your pet's medications with auto-refills, plus online ordering for food and meds.
